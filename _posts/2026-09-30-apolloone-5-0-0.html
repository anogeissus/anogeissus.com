--- layout: post title: "ApolloOne 5.0.0" date: 2026-09-30 11:45:00 +0000 summary: "Tabs and multiple windows, Liquid Glass Mode, ML Processing, Focus Scores, Stacks, AI Rating and AI Keywords, hierarchical keywords and faster imports." ---
  • NEW! Multi-tab / Multi-window support.
  • NEW! Liquid Glass Mode — the photo under a see-through toolbar with glass controls, and a tab overview with the photo of every tab.
  • NEW! List View — the Contact Sheet as a sortable table of columns.
  • NEW! ML Processing — Apple's machine learning RAW decoder.
  • NEW! Process Focus Scores — grade a folder on how sharply the camera focused, with neural network vision processing on faces.
  • NEW! Stacks — group a burst or related shots into one pile.
  • NEW! AI Rating — Apple Private Cloud Compute rates each photo for keep-worthiness, combined with its Focus Score, and picks the key photo of each stack.
  • NEW! AI Keywords — Apple Private Cloud Compute proposes the subject, species, place and keywords of each photo, for you to review and apply.
  • NEW! Hierarchical keywords — a keyword tree with synonyms and categories, read by Lightroom and Photo Mechanic.
  • NEW! Code replacements — short codes and variables expand into full text when saving metadata or renaming.
  • NEW! AppleScript automation — read and write image metadata from scripts, on the whole selection at once.
  • NEW! Hide menu items — hide a function you never use, or a whole menu, and its shortcut key goes with it.
  • NEW! Customizable status bar — choose, arrange and resize the fields along the bottom edge of the window.
  • NEW! Click to zoom replaces the loupe — click or hold the photo to zoom in.
  • NEW! Backup to: — a second copy of every import, in the same run.
  • Importing from a memory card is up to 2× faster.
