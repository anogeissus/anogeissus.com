--- layout: post title: "ApolloOne 5.0.1" date: 2026-10-08 16:16:00 +0000 summary: "Edition tags in the menus, click to zoom can be turned off, a crash at launch on macOS 14 and 15 fixed, and HLG HEIF photos in SDR Proof Mode now match the export." ---
  • Menu items for features in a higher edition now show a Standard, Pro or Max tag.
  • Click to zoom can now be turned off — uncheck every Single click level in "Settings... > Advanced".
  • Fixed a crash at launch on macOS 14 and 15.
  • Fixed HLG HEIF photos in SDR Proof Mode — Quick Edit changes now look the same on screen and in the exported file.
  • Various enhancements and bug fixes.
