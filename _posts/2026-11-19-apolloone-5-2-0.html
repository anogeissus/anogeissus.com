--- layout: post title: "ApolloOne 5.2.0" date: 2026-11-19 00:11:00 +0000 summary: "LUTs and Recipes for photos and videos, Fujifilm film simulations in SDR and HDR, Filmic Curve and Gamut Mapping settings, and better HDR printing." ---
  • NEW! LUTs for photos and videos (Pro Edition) — give a photo or video a look from a .cube LUT in Quick Edit. Hover over a LUT to see it live, even while the video plays, and import whole LUT packs, such as your camera maker's Log LUTs. Use Fujifilm's free LUTs to give RAW files from any camera a Fujifilm film simulation, such as Provia or Classic Chrome, in SDR and HDR; see "Fujifilm Film Simulations" in Help.
  • Starter LUTs (Pro Edition) — film looks, black and white, infrared and more, ready to use in the Starter category of Quick Edit's LUTs page.
  • NEW! Recipes (Pro Edition) — save a LUT with your Quick Edit adjustments as a recipe and use it on other photos. In Contact Sheet mode, a recipe or LUT you click goes on all the selected photos.
  • Default LUT (Pro Edition) — choose a LUT, a film simulation or a recipe under "RAW > Default LUT", and every RAW file without a LUT of its own shows with it, exports included.
  • Enhanced Apple RAW Engine — enables superior highlight recovery for working in Standard Dynamic Range.
  • Filmic Curve on or off (Pro Edition) — turn off ApolloOne's tone curve for a flatter RAW to work from, or when a LUT brings its own contrast.
  • Gamut Mapping on or off (Pro Edition) — check it when bright LED light turns into one flat color in HDR Mode, or uncheck it when neon looks too pale.
  • Improved printing of HDR photos — highlights keep their detail, and prints include the photo's edits and LUT.
  • "Set EXIF Digitized Date..." opens with the photo's date — with one photo selected, change only the part you need instead of typing the whole date.
  • List of variables (Max Edition) — type {} in a metadata or rename field to choose a variable from a list, or click Variables… in the Edit Metadata panel.
  • Undo Reset — choose "Edit > Undo Reset" to get Quick Edit's adjustments back after clicking [Reset], while the image is still on screen.
  • Rename in Split View — double-click the file name at the top of the left or right view.
  • Thumbnail preview in List View — hover over a row's thumbnail to see a larger picture beside it, in HDR for HDR photos.
  • Smoother scrolling with HDR thumbnails — the Contact Sheet and List View no longer stutter.
  • More video formats — .m2ts, .ts, .vob and .mxf videos now open and play. MXF videos need Apple's Pro Video Formats, installed with Final Cut Pro.
  • The Map Info page fits the Inspector again — the map's zoom buttons and compass no longer slip below the bottom edge.
  • Ratings, labels and picks now update every tab showing the same photos.
  • Improved Contrast slider — lowering it now softens faces and brings bright areas down, HDR highlights included.
  • Slightly more natural colors in RAW files.
  • Standard Edition: Sony, OM/Olympus, compressed Fujifilm and Nikon HE/HE* RAW files now open with Apple's RAW decoder on macOS 27.
  • Various enhancements and bug fixes.
