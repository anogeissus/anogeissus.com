--- layout: post title: "ApolloOne GO 2.0.0" date: 2026-10-21 19:04:00 +0000 summary: "Fujifilm Wi-Fi in beta, imports that keep running while you work, a RAW and JPG/HIF filter, laptop layouts for iPhone Duo and iPad, and touch-and-hold zoom." ---
  • NEW! Fujifilm Wi-Fi (beta) — browse, view, share and import photos from a Fujifilm camera without a cable. GO wakes the camera over Bluetooth and joins its Wi-Fi for you. For cameras with X-Processor 5 (tested on X-H2S, X100VI, GFX100S II and X-T5). How to set it up.
  • Keep working while you import — tap Hide, and a green ring in the sidebar shows the progress. Tap it to bring the import back.
  • A folder's Filter menu can show RAW + JPG/HIF, RAW only, or JPG/HIF only.
  • Pick, Reject and Stacks apply to both files of a RAW + JPG/HIF pair — from either one.
  • iPhone Duo — half-fold it like a laptop and the photo has the upper screen to itself, with every control below and a pad to zoom and move around.
  • Laptop mode for iPad — turn it on in the Viewer settings and an iPad held upright gets the same layout.
  • Touch and hold to zoom — hold still on the photo to zoom in, drag to look around, lift to go back.
  • New Viewer settings for double-tap and touch-and-hold zoom — 100% to 400%, the same size for photos of any resolution. Double-tap can be turned off.
  • iPad and iPhone Duo held upright: landscape photos now sit clear of the controls.
  • Fixed Nikon RAW files looking slightly darker than in Photos — on iOS and iPadOS 26 and later.
  • New Help pages — with a contents list in Getting Started and the FAQ, and What's New in Settings.
