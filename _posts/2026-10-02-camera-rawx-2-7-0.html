--- layout: post title: "Camera RawX 2.7.0" date: 2026-10-02 18:15:00 +0000 summary: "Quick Look now matches ApolloOne 5, including its Quick Edit RAW adjustments, and HDR previews roll off bright highlights instead of clipping them." ---
  • OM SYSTEM PEN RAW files now preview with Apple’s RAW engine.
  • Quick Look now matches ApolloOne 5, including the RAW adjustments from its Quick Edit panel: noise reduction, moiré reduction, sharpness, detail and contrast.
  • HDR previews now roll off bright highlights instead of clipping them.
  • On macOS 27, OM SYSTEM OM-1 Mark II, OM-3 and OM-5 Mark II previews use Apple’s own camera profiles.
  • Hasselblad X2D II 100C previews now use the camera’s recorded exposure.
  • Faster decoding of compressed Fujifilm RAW files.
  • Various enhancements and bug fixes.
