--- layout: post title: "ApolloOne GO 1.2.0" date: 2026-08-27 16:41:00 +0000 summary: "Navigation buttons on iPhone in portrait, the controls moved down for a bigger photo, and zoomed navigation that keeps your zoom and position." ---
  • Navigation buttons are now available on iPhone in portrait orientation — above Pick/Reject.
  • The Pick/Reject and navigation buttons on iPhone are smaller, and sit at the bottom of the screen in both orientations — maximum viewing area for the photo. With the navigation buttons off, Pick/Reject moves up within easy thumb reach.
  • Zoomed in, the navigation buttons and arrow keys keep your zoom and position — move to the next or previous image at the same magnification, perfect for comparing sharpness across a burst.
  • NEW! Hide the filename and shooting info while the controls are shown — a new Viewer setting, so nothing sits over your subject on a small screen.
  • Remove a folder's access directly from its long-press menu.
  • Choosing a file instead of a folder in the folder picker now shows a clear message — instead of silently doing nothing.
  • Various enhancements and bug fixes.
