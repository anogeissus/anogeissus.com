--- layout: post title: "ApolloOne GO 1.1.0" date: 2026-08-10 18:45:00 +0000 summary: "JPEG and HEIF photos now appear when no RAW sits beside them, AF points and faces on every format over USB, and FAT32 cards reconnect with one tap." ---
  • JPEG and HEIF (HIF) photos now appear when no RAW of the same name sits beside them — so pre-release capture bursts, which some cameras record as JPEG only, can be browsed and culled like any RAW. RAW+JPEG pairs still show as a single image.
  • AF points and detected faces now display for JPEG and HEIF photos too.
  • AF point and face overlays now work for every supported RAW format when browsing a connected camera or memory card — previously Fujifilm only — including Nikon ZR auto-area AF points.
  • Portrait shots now display upright when browsing a connected camera.
  • Full-resolution previews when browsing Nikon Z and other cameras over USB — no more small, soft images stretched to full screen.
  • FAT32 memory cards (32 GB and smaller) now reconnect with a single tap — keeping your import history and review positions.
  • Various enhancements and bug fixes.
