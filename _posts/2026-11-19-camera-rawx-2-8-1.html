--- layout: post title: "Camera RawX 2.8.1" date: 2026-11-19 00:57:00 +0000 summary: "Quick Look now matches ApolloOne 5.2, bright highlights roll off to white with HDR off, and RAW previews have slightly more natural colors." ---
  • Quick Look now matches ApolloOne 5.2, including its new Contrast slider and the Filmic Curve setting.
  • With HDR off, bright highlights now roll off to white instead of clipping, so warm tones no longer turn yellow and blown skies stay white.
  • Slightly more natural colors in RAW previews.
