ApolloOne GO is now on the App Store, and it answers a request we have had from ApolloOne users for years: the ApolloOne workflow, on the device already in your bag.

Culling is the part of photography that happens away from the desk. On the drive back, in the hotel, in a hide waiting for the light to come round again — the shoot is done and the laptop is not coming out. GO is built for exactly that hour.

  • A real RAW viewer, at full resolution — with HDR display on HDR-capable screens, and the AF points and faces your camera recorded overlaid so you can verify focus at a glance. ISO, aperture, shutter speed and exposure compensation sit under the file name.
  • Browse the camera itself over USB-C — plug the body straight into the iPad and GO reads the card inside it, even on cameras that never mount as a drive. Thumbnails grouped by capture day, full-screen previews with pinch-zoom, select the keepers, and import only those. The rejects never leave the camera.
  • Culling built for your thumbs — green to pick, red to reject, mirrored for left-handed use, and GO remembers your place on every card and folder so you can stop mid-shoot and resume days later.
  • Your picks land on your Mac — every decision is written as a standard macOS Finder color tag and travels through iCloud Drive. Open the folder on your Mac and the decisions are simply there, in Finder or in ApolloOne. No export step, no database, and your RAW files are never modified.
  • Contact sheet, batch operations and AirDrop — filter to picks or rejects, group by capture day, pick or reject whole ranges at once, then copy, move, delete, or AirDrop the originals, their JPG/HIF pairs, or lightweight embedded JPEGs.
  • No import, no library — GO browses folders, external drives and memory cards in place through the Files app.

One thing worth saying plainly, because it decides whether GO is for you: ApolloOne GO displays camera RAW files only. JPEG, HEIC, TIFF and PNG files are not shown. GO culls on the full-size JPEG preview your camera embedded inside each RAW — that is how it swipes through a card at speed — and the Pick and Reject tags belong on the RAW, so they reach your Mac attached to the files you will actually edit. The FAQ covers this and the other questions worth asking before you download.

You do not need a Mac to use GO, and you do not need to use ApolloOne. If Lightroom is your editor, the round trip still works: ApolloOne on the Mac converts GO's Finder tags into XMP as it imports the card, so your picks and rejects arrive in Lightroom with the images.

ApolloOne GO is free to download with a 14-day free trial of every feature, then a one-time purchase to unlock it for good. No subscription, no account, no sign-up. It runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 16 or later.
