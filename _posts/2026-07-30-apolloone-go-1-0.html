ApolloOne GO is now on the App Store — the ApolloOne workflow on your iPad and iPhone, for culling the day's shoot long before the laptop comes out.

  • Full-resolution RAW viewing — HDR display on HDR-capable screens, with AF points and faces overlaid.
  • Browse the camera itself over USB-C — even bodies that never mount as a drive. Pick the keepers, import only those.
  • Culling built for your thumbs — green to pick, red to reject, and GO remembers your place on every card and folder.
  • Your picks land on your Mac — written as standard Finder color tags and synced through iCloud. Your RAW files are never modified.
  • Contact sheet, batch operations and AirDrop — filter, group by capture day, then copy, move, delete or share.
  • Works with Lightroom too — ApolloOne on the Mac converts GO's tags to XMP as it imports the card.
  • RAW files only — JPEG, HEIC, TIFF and PNG are not displayed. GO culls on the full-size preview inside the RAW.
  • Free to download with a 14-day free trial, then a one-time purchase. No subscription. Requires iOS or iPadOS 16 or later.
