--- layout: post title: "ApolloOne 5.1.0" date: 2026-10-22 07:33:00 +0000 summary: "A new Dehaze slider, redesigned Shadows and Contrast sliders, and a bigger tab overview and fixes in Liquid Glass Mode." ---
  • NEW! Dehaze slider — removes haze from distant scenes and brings back their contrast and color.
  • Redesigned Shadows slider — opens up deep shadows that used to stay black, with natural color, less noise and no dark edges along bright skies. Earlier Shadows edits may look different.
  • Redesigned Contrast slider — adds contrast without crushing shadows to black. Earlier Contrast edits may look different.
  • Bigger tab overview in Liquid Glass Mode — previews fill the window, each tab shows its file name, and new zoom buttons set how many tabs fit in a row, from 2 to 8.
  • Liquid Glass Mode fixes — dragging the window by the toolbar no longer zooms the photo, double-clicking the title bar no longer switches to the Contact Sheet, and the status bar no longer covers the photo after leaving full screen.
  • Updated MediaInfo to version 26.10.
  • Various enhancements and bug fixes.
