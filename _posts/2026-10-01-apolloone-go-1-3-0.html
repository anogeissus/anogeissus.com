--- layout: post title: "ApolloOne GO 1.3.0" date: 2026-10-01 01:40:00 +0000 summary: "Stacks, Pick and Reject on RAW + JPEG pairs, a Picked + Unmarked filter, and Delete on memory cards and external drives." ---
  • NEW! Stacks — group a burst into one pile in the Contact Sheet, with Auto-Stack by capture time or from the photos you select. Tap a pile to open it.
  • Pick and Reject now tag a RAW's JPEG or HEIF companion too — so the pair shows the same tag in the Files app and on your memory card.
  • NEW! Picked + Unmarked filter — everything you haven't rejected, ready to copy off the card in one pass.
  • Delete now works on memory cards and external drives — it moves the files into a Rejected folder on the card or drive.
  • Import and Copy/Move now check the free space before they start — and stop at the first file that fails, naming the file and the reason.
  • Importing to an external drive no longer fails with a false out-of-space error.
  • JPEG and HEIF thumbnails no longer show the black bars many cameras add.
  • Sony α7 V lossy compressed RAW files now show thumbnails and previews.
  • Various enhancements and bug fixes.
