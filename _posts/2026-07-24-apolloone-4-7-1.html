--- layout: post title: "ApolloOne 4.7.1" date: 2026-07-24 20:15:00 +0000 summary: "Fixes HDR JPEG exports failing to open on macOS 26 Tahoe, Live Photo renaming, and more." ---
  • Fixed HDR JPEG exports failing to open when exported on macOS 26 Tahoe — JPEGs exported with an HDR gain map on macOS 26 Tahoe could not be displayed by Finder, Preview, or other apps. Files exported on earlier versions of macOS were not affected.
  • Renaming a Live Photo now renames its paired video too — single or batch rename keeps the photo and its video linked, so they continue to move, copy, and delete together.
  • The Export save dialog again warns before replacing an existing file.
  • Fixed sideways Finder thumbnails on portrait photos exported from RAW files.
  • Fixed exported images recording the wrong image size in their metadata on macOS 26 Tahoe.
  • Fixed videos starting to play in the background while in Contact Sheet mode.
  • Various enhancements and bug fixes.

If you exported HDR JPEGs on macOS 26 Tahoe with ApolloOne 4.7.0, those files can be repaired by simply exporting them again with 4.7.1.
