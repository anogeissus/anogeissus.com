--- layout: post title: "ApolloOne 4.8.0" date: 2026-08-12 22:50:00 +0000 summary: "One-click SDR view of HDR photos, true-to-export SDR proofing, a Pick button for the toolbar, embedded JPEG export. Various enhancements and bug fixes." ---
  • NEW! See the SDR version of an HDR photo with one click — a JPEG or HEIC with a gain map carries a second, SDR image inside it: the version viewers without an HDR screen see, on the web too. Press the "→ SDR" button to view that exact image, read straight from the file, and press it again to jump back to HDR for an instant comparison.
  • SDR Proof Mode is now a true proof — the proof preview, the plain SDR export, and the SDR image inside an HDR export are all produced by the same rendering pipeline, so what you proof is exactly what every viewer gets, with any Quick Edit adjustment applied.
  • NEW! Pick button for the toolbar — add Pick to the toolbar from "View > Customize Toolbar" and mark your keepers with the mouse. Together with the Rating group (stars and reject) and Previous / Next, you can cull an entire shoot without touching the keyboard.
  • NEW! Export the JPEG embedded in your RAW file — with "RAW > Use Embedded JPEG Preview Image" turned on, exporting a RAW as a full-size JPEG saves the camera's own preview exactly as the camera wrote it, with its original quality and metadata intact. Choose a smaller size or a different format and ApolloOne renders from the RAW as usual.
  • The floating Browser and Inspector now open from the edge of the window — move the pointer to the left or right edge to bring the panel in, instead of anywhere over the panel's area. In Contact Sheet you can now click the thumbnails nearest the edges without a panel sliding in over them.
  • The import dialog remembers the Advanced options pane — if you close the dialog with Advanced open, it opens that way next time, so your advanced settings are applied without having to press the button each import.
  • Nikon ZR AF points are now displayed — auto-area frames from the ZR previously showed no focus points at all.
  • Fixed the Crop tool forgetting your chosen aspect ratio — the default no longer reverts to Original after cropping or after viewing other cropped images.
  • Auto Tone Curve now works correctly in Split View — each image is analyzed independently, and the checkbox applies only to the selected view.
  • Copying Quick Edit adjustments now handles white balance correctly — applying a copied look whose white balance was untouched restores the target image's as-shot values.
  • Fixed the viewer showing an already filtered-out image after changing the Inspector filter.
  • Various enhancements and bug fixes.
