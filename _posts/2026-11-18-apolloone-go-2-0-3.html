--- layout: post title: "ApolloOne GO 2.0.3" date: 2026-11-18 20:20:00 +0000 summary: "RAW photos now match ApolloOne 5.2, bright highlights roll off to white with HDR off, and colors are slightly more natural." ---
  • RAW photos now match ApolloOne 5.2, including its new Contrast slider and the Filmic Curve setting.
  • With HDR off, bright highlights now roll off to white instead of clipping, so warm tones no longer turn yellow and blown skies stay white.
  • Slightly more natural colors in RAW photos.
