What do I get with the Pro Edition?

Pro includes everything in the Standard Edition, and adds:

  • Hide menu items — hide a function you never use, or a whole menu, and its shortcut key goes with it (new in v5).
  • Backup to — a second copy of every import, written in the same run (new in v5).
  • Importing from a memory card is up to 2× faster (new in v5).
  • Split View with synchronized image zooming and scrolling.
  • Browsing History with Split View support.
  • Batch Image Resize and format conversion; copy/move and resize images at the same time.
  • Batch Image Export, including RAW to JPEG conversions.
  • Import files from memory cards with Auto Resume feature.
  • User-customizable Favorite Tags for the Inspector.
  • Filter files by searching metadata tag contents, including RegEx matching.
  • Filter files by existing keywords found in image/video files.
  • Filter files using GPS Distance Measurement and GPS Altitude.
  • Measure distance between two images using GPS coordinates.
  • Synchronize XMP sidecar file to image.
  • Export images in TIFF, PNG and HEIC file formats.
  • Record slideshow output to a video file.
  • Attach or replace metadata using templates.
  • User customizable shortcut keys.
  • RawBridge™: Apple RAW Engine support for Fujifilm compressed and Nikon HE/HE* compressed RAW files.
  • Autosave / autoreload Quick Edit parameters.
  • AirDrop and drag-and-drop images to another app with InstaBake™ technology.
  • HDR Mode for RAW files — full sensor dynamic range display on XDR/EDR screens.
  • HDR Slideshow — display HDR images at full HDR brightness during slideshow.
  • SDR Proof Mode — live WYSIWYG preview of HDR RAW as plain SDR export.
  • JPEG XL export — HDR and SDR, lossless and lossy compression.
  • AVIF export — HDR PQ 10-bit and SDR 8-bit, YUV 4:4:4 chroma.
  • JPEG and HEIC HDR gain map export.
  • HDR Histogram, multi-band overexposure visualizer and EV readout.

What do I get with the Max Edition?

Max includes everything in the Standard and Pro Editions, and adds:

  • Tabs and multiple windows — browse several folders at the same time, each tab with its own selection, filters and sort order.
  • ML Processing — Apple's machine learning RAW decoder, with machine learning noise reduction and an improved X-Trans demosaic (macOS 27 or later, for supported cameras).
  • Focus Score — grade a folder on how sharply the camera focused, at the camera's own autofocus points and on detected faces, then filter the images by the result.
  • Stacks — group a burst, a bracket or a panorama sequence into one pile in the Contact Sheet.
  • Hierarchical keywords — a keyword tree with synonyms and categories, written to the fields Lightroom and Photo Mechanic read.
  • Code Replacements — short codes and variables expand into full text when saving metadata or renaming files.
  • AI Keywords — Apple Private Cloud Compute proposes the subject, species, place and keywords of each photo, for you to review and apply (macOS 27 or later).
  • AI Rating — Apple Private Cloud Compute rates your photos from 1 to 5 on their merit as pictures and writes the star rating (macOS 27 or later).
  • Customizable status bar — choose, arrange and resize the fields along the bottom edge of the window.
  • AppleScript automation — read and write image metadata from scripts, on the whole selection at once.

Every installation begins with a 14-day Max Edition free trial. Nothing is charged when it ends; ApolloOne reverts to the Free Edition until you choose a subscription or a perpetual license.

We do not quote prices here. Open the purchase window in ApolloOne and the App Store returns the price for your own country, in your own currency, with local taxes and fees already applied.

I own a Standard or Pro perpetual license. How do I move to Max?

There is an upgrade price for you. ApolloOne knows which license you already own, so it applies the reduced price itself. Choose ApolloOne > Purchase Max Edition Perpetual License… and the price shown is the one for your license, returned by the App Store in your own currency for your own country.

To try it first, ApolloOne > 14-Day Max Edition Free Trial… gives you the Max Edition for two weeks, with nothing charged when it ends.

The upgrade price is for perpetual owners. A Standard or Pro subscription does not qualify for it; see the next question for how subscriptions work.

I subscribe to the Standard or Pro Edition. What do I do now?

There is nothing to cancel first. Standard, Pro and Max sit in one subscription group, so choosing ApolloOne > 1-Year Max Subscription… is an upgrade within it. Apple stops your current subscription, refunds the unused part of it, and starts Max straight away, so you are not paying for two editions at once.

You can also stay where you are. Your Standard or Pro subscription keeps working as before, and it covers the same features in Version 5 that it covers today. The Max features are additions on top of it.

I purchased version 1.x, do I have to pay again for version 5?

There is no need to pay again. You receive a perpetual Standard Edition license in version 5, and every Standard Edition feature is available to you. Version 5 also starts with a 14-day Max Edition free trial, so you can try the Pro and Max features before deciding.

I purchased version 2.x, do I have to pay again for version 5?

For version 2 paid users, you receive a perpetual license for the Standard Edition in version 5. Every feature you had in version 2 is still there, along with the new Standard Edition features. You can explore the Pro and Max features by starting the 14-day Max Edition free trial.

I purchased version 2.x, and if I subscribed to the Pro Edition for one year and decided not to continue with the subscription for the next year, do I get my Standard Edition Perpetual License back?

YES! When you decide not to renew, your Standard Edition Perpetual License comes back. We continue to improve ApolloOne, and new features arrive in the Max Edition first.

Can I use the free version indefinitely?

Sure you can! However, a message will popup reminding you to purchase a subscription after using the free version for 14 days. We hope you enjoy using ApolloOne!

ApolloOne asks me to purchase again after moving to a new Mac

  • Your license is tied to your Apple ID, not the old Mac.
  • Choose ApolloOne > Restore Purchase from the menu bar.
  • Make sure the App Store is signed in with the same Apple ID you used for the original purchase.
  • Still locked out? Contact us with your original purchase date.

Working with NAS

Apple has discontinued the support for AFP (Apple Filing Protocol) since macOS 10.9. Apple currently recommend SMB networking protocol when connecting to a NAS device. Current AFP implementation (up and including macOS Monterey and Ventura) is very buggy and can cause ApolloOne to appear to hang when accessing a NAS folder.

To work around this bug, you need to remove all network folders you added in ApolloOne. Then login to your NAS and enable SMB protocol (if not already enabled). Then disable AFP protocol. You may lose connection to your NAS once AFP is disabled. You will need to reconnect to the NAS using Finder and macOS will use the SMB protocol instead.

Once AFP is disabled, you can add your network folders back to Places in ApolloOne.

Working with JPEG XL Files

ApolloOne may not be able to display metadata with certain JPEG XL files. You need to install CPAN Brotli module in order to decode these JPEG XL metadata.

To do so, open Terminal, and type:

sudo cpan -i IO::Uncompress::Brotli

You will be asked for your login password, assuming you have administrator privilege.

This may take a few minutes to install. Once installation is complete, you should be able to see all the metadata with your JPEG XL files.

Music not playing in Slideshow

Under newer versions of macOS such as Sequoia, you may need to add permission to allow ApolloOne to access the music tracks and playlists in Apple Music. Usually, ApolloOne will prompt you for permission when you start the Slideshow to allow access to Apple Music. If you answered "Don't Allow", you can add this permission back later on under System Settings.

Go to System Settings > Privacy & Security > Media & Apple Music

Add ApolloOne to the list to allow access to Apple Music.
