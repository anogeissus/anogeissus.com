Cameras Supported by ML Processing

ML Processing runs on Apple's machine learning RAW decoder. Apple decides which cameras that decoder covers, and these are the models on the list today.

The list grows with macOS. When Apple adds a camera in a macOS update, ApolloOne picks it up on its own, with nothing to download from us. A RAW file from a camera that is not on the list is decoded normally, at the quality ApolloOne has always given it. What it misses is the machine learning noise reduction and the improved X-Trans demosaic.

Your own Mac has the current answer: in ApolloOne, choose RAW > ML Processing Camera Support List. It reads the camera database of the macOS you are running, so it stays right as Apple extends the list. If your camera is missing here but shows up there, your macOS is newer than this page.

If your camera is not here

Nothing is missing from your copy of ApolloOne, and there is no setting to find. The decoder and the list are Apple's, so we cannot add a camera to it. You can ask Apple for yours through Feedback Assistant on your Mac, which is how the list has grown so far.

In the meantime ApolloOne decodes your RAW files as version 4 did, at the same quality. Every other Version 5 feature works whatever camera you shoot: tabs and windows, Stacks, Focus Score, the keyword tree, AI Keywords and AI Rating.

ML Processing is part of ApolloOne and requires macOS 27 or later and a Max Edition subscription or perpetual license. Back to ApolloOne.
